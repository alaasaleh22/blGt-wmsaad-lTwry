---
name: Emergency-report safety
description: Product claims and demo-data safeguards for emergency reporting features.
---

Emergency report forms must clearly distinguish saving a report from notifying responders. Do not claim that reports are monitored, reviewed, or dispatched unless a real response operation is connected. Do not seed fictitious emergency incidents that could be mistaken for real cases.

**Why:** People may rely on emergency interfaces during life-threatening situations.

**How to apply:** Keep an explicit local-emergency-services warning visible, describe stored statuses accurately, and use empty aggregate counts until real reports exist.
